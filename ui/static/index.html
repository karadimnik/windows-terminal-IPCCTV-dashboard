      Segments

        The index is parsed from filenames — Front_Entrance_20261013_190147+0300.mkv already carries the camera, the date, the time and the UTC offset, so there is no database and nothing opens a segment to list it. A segment plays as MP4 remuxed on request: the video is copied and only the 8 kHz camera audio is re-encoded. The segment being recorded right now plays up to the point it has reached.

        Tiles show each camera's second stream. Click a tile to watch it on the main stream, and click it or press Esc to go back. Sound is off until you unmute a tile. Pictures stop while this tab is hidden or another tab is open, so a hidden page costs the camera and this PC nothing.
